Paper Fact

Know When to Abstain: Conformal Confidence Estimation for Text-to-SQL

Avinash Kumar Singh, Bhaskarjit Sarmah & Stefano Pasquali · arXiv:2611.31190v1 · submitted 23 Nov 2026 · CC BY-NC-ND 4.0

84evidence score
0.899AUARC, main Spider setting
52.4%coverage at 15% risk budget
9.1%mean accepted-set error
30.1%BIRD error using Spider threshold

Contribution and method

The system separates a generator from a critic. A random forest fuses five LLM-derived scores with token uncertainty and SQL structure; a Learn-then-Test threshold controls accepted-set risk with probability 1−δ under the stated sampling assumptions, while a conformal p-value addresses false abstention. Spider uses 3,181 question–SQL pairs over 200 databases, stratified 60/20/20; forests are tuned on training, calibrated on a disjoint split and evaluated on test.

Quantitative trace. Table 3: GPT-OSS-120B generator + Gemma-4-31B critic, ε=.15, δ=.1: 10/10 certified splits, 52.4% coverage, 9.1% mean risk, 11.7% maximum. Without abstention, 22.6% are wrong.
Baseline trace. Table 4 uses the same Olmo-3-32B-Think generator and splits: 89.5 AUARC versus 81.2 SAC-Product, 82.1 five-sample self-consistency and 78.9 two-call CoT verification; bootstrap 95% interval 86.7–91.9.

Methodological critique

The controlled baseline comparison and 10 resampled splits are unusually useful. But the central guarantee is conditional, not portable. BIRD has a different difficulty/base rate: only 34.2% generated queries are correct; AUROC falls from .791 to .724 and the 15% threshold produces 30.1% error. A 10% budget is often uncertifiable with 636 calibration examples. Two sequential LLM calls add about 4.3 seconds per query, and only SQLite/open-weight models are evaluated.

Prior-work comparison

The contribution is not merely another confidence feature. It compares against token-product confidence, pooled token confidence, self-consistency and a same-call-count verifier; the largest gains come from feature fusion. The paper also shows a logit-free variant at 88.6 AUARC, 1.6 points below the ten-seed full model.

Reproducibility. Datasets, splits, features and hyperparameters are detailed, but no verified code repository is identified in the paper or primary record. Reproducibility: moderate.

Enterprise readiness. 3/5 for shadow gating on a well-labeled stable workflow; 1/5 for cross-domain guarantees. Required internal context: error cost, minimum viable coverage, label latency, schema/model update rate, critic independence and reviewer capacity.

Paper Fact

TrialAgentBench: Evaluating AI Agents for Clinical-Trial Analysis and Long-Horizon Drug-Development Decisions

Bradley Segal, William Bolton, Philip Torr, David Clifton & Lei Clifton · arXiv:2611.30916v1 · accepted NeurIPS 2026 E&D Track · CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 paper

94evidence score
3,492public tasks
24models evaluated
30×replication trials per analysis task
48% vs 57.7%best agent vs rule-based value

Contribution and method

TrialEval supplies completed trial documents and raw records; agents save Python/R code which is rerun unchanged. Its 420 public tasks span 17 clinical questions, five context levels and trials from 52 to 114,000 participants. TrialDev contains 3,072 tasks across 384 six-year programmes with 3 or 5 drug candidates, budgets up to US$314M and at least 1,021 study-design combinations. Evaluation uses simulated counterfactual truth hidden from the agent.

Analysis trace. The top five models are 3.5–5.8% above a process-aware analysis with 0–4% serious misses and 94–95% interval coverage. The paper reports improvement from 19–23% higher error and 15–17% serious misses for August 2025 models.
Decision trace. Opus 5.5 reaches 48% of oracle value (95% interval 30–62) versus 57.7% for a rule-based developer. Agents with statistically similar analysis error span 14–48% oracle value; average budget use is only 43%.

Methodological critique

The benchmark validates its simulator against trial sizes, conduct and Cochrane effect distributions; process-aware bias is within .05 standard errors with 92–96% coverage, and task split-half reliability is .895. Saved code is rerun on 30 replications; an independent verifier reproduces decisions and values. Still, synthetic worlds encode chosen clinical-value, harm, operating-cost and prior assumptions. The oracle searches a limited plan grid and is a reference, not an upper bound. Model identities/costs can age quickly, and clinical performance does not authorize care.

Prior-work comparison

Unlike trial benchmarks graded against published labels, this work scores methods against simulated truth and policies against counterfactual development value. The paper documents why an earlier accepted-method grade was inadequate: strict grading marked 28% of answers wrong even when they were as accurate as the process-aware analysis.

Artifacts. Benchmark, environment, replication trials, rule-based developer and independent verifier are released at GitHub; the dataset card and license specify CC BY-NC 4.0, non-commercial research/evaluation, not clinical care or production.

Reproducibility. Strong for benchmark inspection and rerunning; full model evaluations still depend on proprietary models and compute. Reproducibility: high.

Enterprise readiness. 4/5 as an evaluation pattern; 1/5 for autonomous clinical decisions. Required internal context: valid domain simulator, utility/harm model, outcome oracle, sandbox, human authority and regulatory scope.

Preflight: Agentic Last-Mile Verification for AI Software Supply Chains

Yaman Jandali, Nojan Sheybani, Farinaz Koushanfar & Tara Javidi · arXiv:2611.31110v1 · submitted 23 Nov 2026 · CC BY 4.0

77evidence score
4/5malicious incident-context verdicts

Contribution and method

Preflight extracts application-referenced dependency symbols, asks a model to construct validated function/constructor probes, executes exact artifacts as non-root in a restricted Docker environment, reduces traces to canonical behavioral fingerprints, compares target with a defensible predecessor and signs an in-toto/DSSE statement. Static analysis remains complementary; final incident verdicts were deterministic because model-assisted verdict requests exceeded context limits.

Incident trace. LiteLLM client (1.82.6→1.82.7) is clean because the compromised proxy path is not exercised; the same package pair is malicious under a proxy workload. Ultralytics, ctx and target-only DurableTask are malicious. DurableTask’s signal includes sandbox infrastructure and cannot establish a version-introduced change.
Mechanism trace. With artifact, dependencies, environment, timeouts and policy held fixed, function-level probes detect both Ultralytics and ctx incidents; import-only probes detect neither. All 100 curated benign packages complete cleanly, but only exercised paths are measured.

Methodological critique

The paired harness ablation and same-package/different-workload LiteLLM comparison give strong mechanistic evidence. Statistical scope remains small: four incidents, two ablation cases and a curated 100-package benign set. Reflection, plugins, callbacks, dynamic imports, argument states and input-sensitive branches can escape coverage. A prior version is not proof of benignity; target-only behavior is especially hard to attribute. Docker with ptrace is not the strongest containment boundary.

Prior-work comparison

Where provenance tools identify artifacts, static tools flag code and package-level sandboxes observe generic behavior, Preflight conditions dynamic evidence on the downstream program and version differential. It does not establish that LLM-generated probes outperform deterministic probes with equivalent API knowledge.

Artifacts. Exact incident sources and artifacts are documented, but no verified public implementation repository is identified. Paper license: CC BY 4.0.

Reproducibility. Environment and policy are described; implementation and full evaluation corpus are not published as a verified artifact. Reproducibility: moderate-low.

Enterprise readiness. 2/5 for isolated shadow preflight; 0/5 for automatic admission. Required internal context: languages/package managers, lock discipline, isolation, outbound policy, allowable latency, signing, exception process and incident owner.

Cross-paper synthesis

Three separations define the control plane

Capability ≠ authority

Agents can write good analyses or useful tools without earning release, stop or high-consequence decision authority.

Confidence ≠ validity

A ranking can remain informative after shift while its risk guarantee has already failed. Monitor assumptions, not only scores.

Artifact ≠ behavior

A package or script should be replayed through the paths and cases that matter, under independent observation and rollback.

Independent Analysis. The common enterprise pattern is an evidence plane that observes behavior and outcomes, a policy plane that decides whether evidence is valid enough, and an authorization plane that controls whether anything consequential may happen.

Knowledge reinforcement

Five rules worth carrying forward

  1. A calibrated threshold expires when its exchangeability assumptions expire.
  2. Rerun executable artifacts on new cases; do not score only the final answer.
  3. Application-conditioned behavior is stronger security evidence than package identity alone.
  4. Accurate local analysis does not imply good long-horizon decisions.
  5. Generated tools require admission, provenance, least privilege and revocation—not just tests.

Retrieval practice

Why did the SQL guarantee fail on BIRD?

The target queries were not exchangeable with Spider. Ranking remained informative, but the certified threshold no longer controlled risk.

What makes TrialAgentBench unusually decision-useful?

It reruns saved code on replication trials and scores sequential policies against simulated truth and downstream value, exposing counterfactual decisions.

What does 0/100 false positives mean for Preflight?

Only that none were observed on 100 curated packages under the exercised workloads. It is not proof of a zero population false-positive rate or complete path coverage.

Why is ToolSmith not Prototype Soon?

Modest VLM-judged gains coexist with incorrect generated tools, drifting live APIs and a process-level sandbox that is not sufficient for enterprise execution.

One-sentence synthesis. A trustworthy enterprise AI system should know when its evidence no longer applies, prove what its artifacts do on new cases, and keep consequential authority outside the model.
