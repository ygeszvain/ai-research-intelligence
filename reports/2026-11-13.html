Evidence-grounded brief · November 13, 2026

A transcript is not state.

Today’s strongest papers converge on one enterprise rule: bind every agent claim to an observed world effect, and every evaluator to the boundary where consequences become real. Memory repair, judge scores, red-team success, and compiler speedups all become misleading when the measured record diverges from the system that will actually execute.

Executive intelligence

What changed—and what it means

Paper facts, independent analysis, enterprise inference, and uncertainty are explicitly separated. Nothing below is a production-deployment approval.

Paper Fact

History edits can reverse sign

With the world held wrong, a truthful note raised AgentCE-Bench recovery from roughly 51% to 66%, while a record that falsely depicted the correction as completed reduced recovery from roughly 50% to 44%. On AppWorld, a success receipt helped only when the effect actually existed.

Paper Fact

Stable judges can still measure the wrong thing

Across 2,160 tau2-bench episodes, an LLM judge repeated itself 98.2% of the time but agreed with native scoring only 56.2%. A deterministic write-boundary check caught all 11 forbidden writes in the human-labeled sample.

Paper Fact

Stronger attack search improved robust training

OpenAI reports a controlled RL comparison in which robustified data raised safety on held-out fixed jailbreaks from 45% to 68% and on adaptive online attacks from 71% to 92%. Worst@n reduces variance, not systematic judge bias.

Independent Analysis

Decision headline

The highest-leverage near-term work is not another agent framework. It is a consequence-aware control plane: canonical effect receipts, write-boundary policies, evidence-preserving context, evaluator disagreement telemetry, and reversible shadow evaluation.

Uncertainty

Why the evidence is not deployment approval

Agent-state studies use selected benchmarks and mostly closed models; the evaluator study has one domain pair and a 120-episode human sample; OpenAI’s core models, data, and training stack are not publicly reproducible; and Watchlist compiler gains are workload- and hardware-specific.

Paper Fact

Cleaning an Agent’s History Hurts When the World Still Holds the Error

Pakhomov et al. · Salesforce AI Research · arXiv:2611.14654v1 · 10 Nov 2026 · CC BY 4.0

Research score92/100
Controlled intervention319 AgentCE states65 AppWorld familiesAncillary code/dataClosed receivers

Question and design

The paper freezes an agent mid-task with the exact world state, then edits either the history or world before a receiver continues. AgentCE uses a four-arm design around a committed wrong value; AppWorld crosses receipt success/failure with effect present/absent. This directly separates “the record says it happened” from “the world contains the effect.”

Quantitative claim trace

+15.20 ppTruthful note versus matched neutral note on the look-ahead endpoint; 95% interval [12.46, 18.00].
−6.43 ppFalsely completed correction versus unedited record; 95% interval [−8.88, −3.97].
27 pp interactionOn AppWorld’s headline means, success receipt changes whole-task success from −17 pp when the effect is absent (45%→28%) to +11 pp when present (38%→49%).

Methodological critique

  • Strong: world restoration, co-started arms, task-family bootstrap, prewritten contrasts, receiver expansion, and zero-model-call recomputation from frozen records.
  • Confound: truth, role/channel, position, retained error, and unfinished-work framing are not fully crossed; the truthful note bundles several features.
  • Generalization: only two benchmarks; 319 states use two receivers; AppWorld uses 65 task families and eight receivers; all receivers are mutable closed APIs.
  • Protocol deviation: two preregistered scripts refused to run and reported figures came from checked reimplementations with the failing gate removed.

Prior-work delta

Earlier history-repair work typically cleaned a record while holding the world fixed and did not independently manipulate claimed completion and actual effect. The contribution is the crossed causal treatment of record and world.

ArtifactsReproducibilityEnterprise readiness
Ancillary bundle includes frozen records, arm packets, manifests, prompts, outputs, hashes, and scripts; paper says every readout is recomputed without model calls.High for reported readouts; lower for fresh continuation due closed models, costs, and version drift.High for shadow validation of memory/compaction; low for automatic production mutation without effect verification and internal privacy review.

Enterprise Inference Treat a history edit as a state transition requiring a verified effect receipt—not as text cleanup. Internal context needed before implementation: tool semantics, transaction boundaries, current audit lineage, retry/idempotency behavior, regulated data constraints, and acceptable false-block rate.

Paper Fact

When Evaluators of AI Agents Disagree

Im · aSSIST / AIM Intelligence · arXiv:2611.14581v1 · 10 Nov 2026 · arXiv license

Research score90/100
2,160 tau2-bench episodesFour evaluators120 human-labeledZenodo DOIGate ablation

Question and design

The study runs airline and retail agents under four gate conditions, records each database write, and scores every episode with native state scoring, an LLM judge, a policy-informed judge, and a minimal expected-write/observed-write evaluator. Two annotators independently label 120 stratified episodes on task correctness, policy compliance, and commitment correctness.

Quantitative claim trace

98.2% vs 56.2%Judge self-agreement [97.5%, 98.8%] versus judge/native agreement [49.6%, 62.7%]; stability did not imply construct alignment.
11/11 caughtThe boundary evaluator and native scorer caught all human-confirmed forbidden writes; small judges passed 10–11, and the strongest no-policy judge passed 6.
No significant gate effectAfter Holm correction across eight tests, verification/correction/safe-termination gates did not significantly reduce premature commitments.

Methodological critique

  • Strong: same episodes across evaluators, task-cluster bootstrap, task-level permutation tests, multiple-comparison correction, blinded axes, full released traces and adjudication.
  • Reference limits: one annotator is the author and the other was a colleague; policy-axis raw agreement was only 0.650 before adjudication.
  • External validity: only two tau2-bench domains, 24 episodes per task, four model/gate settings, and a stratified—not simple random—human sample.
  • Gate implementation: digest truncation dropped order status and authentication; the negative gate result measures the implemented gate, not an ideal verifier.

Prior-work delta

Judge-reliability studies usually compare judges to human labels or each other. This study aligns transcript judges, state scorers, a deterministic commit oracle, and human constructs on the same tool traces.

Paper Fact

Improving Fuzzy Robustness in LLM Safety with Automated Red Teaming

Wang et al. · OpenAI · 12 Nov 2026 · 20-page technical paper

Research score87/100
Automated red teamingWorst@nControlled RL comparisonAdaptive attackersClosed artifacts

Question and design

An attacker iteratively changes a seed task, queries one or more defenders, and receives safety-judge feedback. Worst@n accepts an attack only when all n sampled defender responses and judgments mark success. A controlled post-training comparison starts from the same checkpoint and training mixture, with the treatment adding robustified biology and cybersecurity tasks.

Quantitative claim trace

45%→68%Safety robustness on held-out fixed jailbreaks in the controlled comparison.
71%→92%Safety robustness against adaptive online attacks.
−5.0 pp utilityAppendix cybersecurity utility fell from 81.7% [78.7,84.4] to 76.7% [73.5,79.7], despite small safety gains on the non-adversarial evaluation.

Methodological critique

  • Strong: separate search and independent evaluation, held-out defender transfer, adaptive multi-turn attacks, controlled initialization, Wilson intervals, and explicit utility checks.
  • Judge risk: repeated sampling suppresses variance but not systematic grader bias; authors report manually discovering and patching judge gaps.
  • Reproducibility: model identities are partly internal; training data, weights, harness, prompts, and per-condition counts are not released in a runnable package.
  • Scope: controlled evidence centers on biology/cyber safety and one offline robustification round; production safeguards were intentionally excluded from core model comparisons.

Prior-work delta

The harness extends PAIR-style iterative refinement. The distinctive contribution is using a repeated unanimous success objective during search, then demonstrating transfer and defender-training gains under stronger adaptive evaluation.

Paper, blog, and system-card results; no public training dataset, weights, or executable harness verified.Low externally for training; moderate for conceptual replication with an organization’s own models and policies.Medium for isolated red-team evaluation; low for automatic training-data ingestion without expert review, access controls, and misuse safeguards.

Enterprise Inference Repeated independent attack success is a useful acceptance rule for red-team candidates, but expert review must remain between attack generation and any training or production change. Internal context needed: risk taxonomy, model access, evaluation policy, data retention, dual-use controls, compute budget, and security review.

Paper Fact

Together Compiler: Continual Compilation for AI Inference

Agent hill-climbing, a tiered persistent engine, and a parallelism cost model reportedly produce up to 1.7× TPS over hand-written Llama-1B megakernels and up to 2.6× over best open InferenceX results, with 252× higher experiment throughput and 11× lower agent idle time in a reported setting.

Why watch: production-scale claim, broad hardware/model tests, bit-exact checks, and unusually useful ablations. Real data exposed a top-k kernel that was 1.3× slower end-to-end despite looking good on random data.

Why not deep priority: no runnable compiler package verified; vendor-authored benchmarks, heterogeneous baselines, “up to” maxima, hardware dependence, and limited statistical reporting make independent reproduction difficult.

Uncertainty The production statement (“trillions of tokens per day”) and economics cannot be independently audited from the paper.

Paper Fact

Abstention Is Not Threshold Compliance

Across 12 open 3–8B checkpoints, an “answer or I abstain” format raised mean abstention from 20% to 91% at t=.75, yet changing t=.5→.9 altered abstention by −0.8 points inside that format. External recalibration made coverage monotonic by construction but transferred imperfectly.

Why watch: prespecified tests, 12 checkpoints across post-training stages, open-model outputs, MIT code, CC BY results, and a hashed change manifest.

Why not deep priority: short factual QA, mostly 3–8B open models, greedy decoding, one rule wording, and weak cross-dataset calibration transfer.

Enterprise Inference “The prompt says answer above 0.8” is not a control. A separate calibrated decision service is more defensible, but must be recalibrated under drift.

Cross-paper synthesis

Five patterns that survive paper boundaries

Independent Analysis Grounded only in the reviewed papers and verified artifacts.

1. Records are lossy views of consequences

Agent histories, judge transcripts, compressed evidence digests, and synthetic compiler inputs all omit causal state. Safe systems must preserve or re-query the effect-bearing source of truth.

2. Reliability is not validity

A judge can be 98.2% self-consistent yet disagree with state scoring. An attack can pass once because of noisy grading. Repetition measures variance; it does not prove that the construct is right.

3. Boundary-specific oracles beat universal prose

Expected-write versus observed-write checks catch a crisp failure that broad LLM judges miss. External thresholds can enforce monotonic abstention. Bit-exact tests can reject compiler candidates. Determinism is most valuable at narrow consequence boundaries.

4. Context engineering can fail in both directions

Dropping critical fields breaks gates; adding expert knowledge can slow compiler agents; rewriting history can hide an unresolved effect. Context should be tested as a causal component, not assumed beneficial.

5. Deployment evidence must include negatives

The strongest papers report reversals, null gate effects, failed transfer, utility regression, or real-data slowdowns. Enterprise experiments should predeclare both success and stop conditions.

Unifying architecture principle

Generative proposal → evidence-preserving context → deterministic boundary check → authorized action → observed effect receipt → multi-view evaluation → human escalation. No single transcript or score should stand in for this chain.

Implementation priorities

A reversible 90-day sequence

No production mutation is required to learn from today’s research.

Days 0–30

Define boundaries

  • Inventory one tool-using workflow and its state-changing calls.
  • Name authoritative state, effect, policy, identity, and compensation sources.
  • Specify critical context fields and canonical action/effect events.
  • Sample 120–200 historical episodes for blind human labels.
Days 31–60

Shadow and replay

  • Emit append-only receipts in a sandbox adapter.
  • Run world–history and expected-write checks without blocking.
  • Compare transcript, state, boundary, and human verdicts.
  • Audit prompt-stated thresholds and current digest field recall.

Knowledge reinforcement

Recall the decision logic

Six questions for leadership and engineering review.

1. Why can “cleaning” memory hurt?

Answer: if the edit says an action succeeded while the world still holds its error, the receiver may trust the false completion and skip recovery.

2. Why does 98.2% judge repeatability not prove quality?

Answer: the same judge agreed with native state scoring only 56.2%; it may consistently measure a different construct.

3. What is the right place for deterministic rules?

Answer: narrow consequence boundaries—writes, authorization, idempotency, schema completeness, bit-exactness—not every ambiguous policy question.

4. What does worst@n fix?

Answer: repeated unanimous success reduces variance and search-time false positives; it does not eliminate biased graders.

5. Why is prompt-only abstention deprioritized?

Answer: high abstention did not imply decisions tracked the stated threshold; external calibrated enforcement is more defensible.

6. What justifies roadmap promotion?

Answer: internal replication on at least two workflows, measurable end-to-end benefit, acceptable false blocks and cost, security/privacy approval, named owners, and reversible rollout.

Evidence-label policy

  • Paper Fact Directly reported by a reviewed source.
  • Independent Analysis Cross-source synthesis or scoring by this brief.
  • Enterprise Inference Evidence-grounded option requiring internal validation.
  • Uncertainty Missing, ambiguous, unverified, or externally limited evidence.

Research standard: primary-source-first, quantitative claim tracing, explicit limitations, artifact/license checks, and no production recommendation from novelty or benchmark performance alone.
