Across the evidence

Cross-paper synthesis

1 · Split the layers

Raw policy support, channel integrity, execution truth, constraint evidence, and outcome are different observables. A single score collapses them.

2 · Bound deterministic checks

Parser-contract tests and domain rules give reproducible evidence for explicit conditions—not semantic truth outside them.

3 · Volume cannot repair bias

More samples did not rescue the unstable observer; larger models did not repair a systematically mismatched tool channel.

4 · Artifacts alter decision value

Runnable repositories enable internal challenge tests. Missing artifacts keep otherwise promising work below implementation threshold.

Knowledge reinforcement

Six durable distinctions

Policy ≠ parserA valid raw call discarded by a parser is interface censoring.
Delivery ≠ reliabilityPerfect HTTP/schema delivery does not make an evaluator repeatable.
Volume ≠ correctionMore samples do not repair systematic mapping bias or drift.
Quality ≠ safetyPredictive accuracy and constraint satisfaction are separate axes.
Rules ≠ truthDeterministic checks only cover the conditions they encode.
Experiment ≠ rolloutWrite the success and stop conditions before testing.
